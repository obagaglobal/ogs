## 1. Goal

Customers (individuals, and organisation members with `ticket.create`) **open support tickets**, talk with OGS through a public conversation with attachments, and see progress. OGS helpdesk staff **triage, take, prioritise, reply, keep internal notes, resolve and close** tickets, and can open tickets **on behalf of** a customer who phoned, emailed or walked in.

Review §13 sets the acceptance bar:

- internal notes are absent from every customer endpoint (contract test);
- the activity log is complete;
- the reopen policy is enforced.

Phase 8 stops before field work: dispatching a ticket to a technician or provider (`ServiceAssignment`) is Phase 9, and SLA timers are Phase 10. Phase 8 records the timestamps Phase 10 will need.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Data model

Ticket rows carry `customer_tenant_id` **or** `customer_user_id` (ADR-009), with a CHECK requiring exactly one.

| Table                  | Purpose                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ticket`               | Number `TCK-2026-000123`. Subject, description, type (INCIDENT, QUESTION), category, requested urgency and staff-set priority (P8-3), channel (WEB, PHONE, EMAIL, WALK_IN, WHATSAPP), requester (`requester_user_id`), `department_id` snapshot (P8-4), optional `service_order_id` and `service_id`, optional `related_ticket_id` (follow-up of a closed ticket), assignee (OGS staff, P8-1), status, `version`, `first_response_at`, `resolved_at`, `closed_at`, `reopen_count`, `created_by` and `on_behalf` flag. |
| `ticket_comment`       | **Public** conversation only: author, author side (CUSTOMER / STAFF), body, at. Append-only (a trigger refuses UPDATE and DELETE).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `ticket_internal_note` | Staff-only notes in **their own table** (P8-5). Append-only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `ticket_attachment`    | Ticket, file, and the comment **or** internal note it belongs to (CHECK exactly one). Uses the existing `ticket.attachment` file purpose (`src/files/file-purposes.ts`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `ticket_event`         | Append-only activity log: kind (created, status, priority, category, assignee, link, comment, internal note, attachment), from, to, actor, reason, at. **One row per command.**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `ticket_category`      | Platform-owned, seeded list (Hardware, Software, Network and internet, Email and accounts, Printers, Security incident, Other). Readable by any signed-in context; edited in Phase 14 admin.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

`document_counter` gains the `TCK` kind (`nextNumber` in `src/orders/order-support.ts` moves to a shared place, e.g. `src/common/document-numbers.ts`, and accepts it).

**RLS (all tables enabled and forced, explicit grants to `ogs_app`, entries in `rls-catalogue.int.test.ts` and `isolation.int.test.ts`):**

- `ticket`, `ticket_comment`, public `ticket_attachment` rows, `ticket_event`: `app.owns_customer_row()` (organisation rows in the organisation workspace, a person's rows only in their personal workspace), or the elevated platform branch. Workers get scoped `tickets.*` jobs.
- `ticket_internal_note` and internal attachment rows: **platform branch only**. No customer branch exists, so a customer context cannot read a note even through a bug in the service layer.
- Providers see nothing yet. Phase 9 adds the assignment branch (ADR-010) to `ticket` and, for `ticket.internal_note` holders, to notes.
- `ticket_event` rows for internal notes are written with kind `internal_note` and **no body**; customer views filter that kind out (and the contract test checks it).

### 2.2 Lifecycle (one transition table, `src/tickets/transitions.ts`)

```text
NEW ─staff:triage─▶ OPEN ─staff:start─▶ IN_PROGRESS
IN_PROGRESS ─staff:await customer─▶ AWAITING_CUSTOMER ─customer:reply (automatic)─▶ IN_PROGRESS
IN_PROGRESS ─staff:hold (reason)─▶ ON_HOLD ─staff:resume─▶ IN_PROGRESS
NEW | OPEN | IN_PROGRESS | AWAITING_CUSTOMER | ON_HOLD ─staff:resolve (resolution note)─▶ RESOLVED
RESOLVED ─customer:reopen (reason, within 7 days) or staff:reopen─▶ IN_PROGRESS
RESOLVED ─customer:accept resolution, or system after 7 days─▶ CLOSED
NEW | OPEN | IN_PROGRESS | AWAITING_CUSTOMER | ON_HOLD ─customer:withdraw (reason)─▶ CLOSED (resolution "withdrawn")
CLOSED is final: a follow-up is a new ticket with related_ticket_id.
```

- Same shape as `src/orders/transitions.ts`: each command lists from-states, actor and permission; every command runs in one `TenantDb.run` transaction, checks actor, permission, state and `version`, and writes one `ticket_event`, an audit row and an outbox event.
- A database trigger (`protect_ticket`) is the second guard: customers may only insert tickets and comments, reopen, accept or withdraw; they can never set priority, assignee, `first_response_at` or status other than those moves.
- **Auto-close (P8-2):** resolving schedules a delayed `tickets.auto_close` outbox event at `resolved_at + 7 days`, plus an hourly sweep; both are idempotent, copying `QuotationExpiryService` / the sweeper in `src/orders/quotation-expiry.service.ts`. A reopen in the same instant as the close is decided by `version`.
- `first_response_at` is set by the first public staff comment (internal notes don't count); `resolved_at` and `closed_at` by their transitions; a reopen clears `resolved_at` and increments `reopen_count`.
- Customer labels: Open (NEW, OPEN), In progress (IN_PROGRESS, ON_HOLD), Waiting for you (AWAITING_CUSTOMER), Resolved, Closed.

### 2.3 Customers

- `POST /tickets` with `Idempotency-Key` (reuse `idempotent()` from `order-support.ts`); optional `serviceOrderId` must be one of the customer's own orders (checked in the service **and** by a trigger).
- **Who:** individuals in the personal workspace; organisation members with `ticket.create`. Provider, platform and restricted workspaces are refused.
- **Who sees what (P8-4):** own tickets; `ticket.read_department` adds tickets from the member's department; `ticket.read_all` (org admins) shows all of the organisation's.
- `GET /tickets`, `GET /tickets/:id` (public comments, public attachments, filtered activity), `POST /tickets/:id/comments` (`ticket.comment`; attachments by file id after upload), `POST …/reopen`, `…/accept-resolution`, `…/withdraw`.
- Attachments: upload through the existing FileService with purpose `ticket.attachment`; up to 5 per comment, 10 MB each; a file shows "Scanning…" until clean, and only clean files get a download link.
- Rate limits with the Phase 3 limiter (`src/identity/rate-limiter.ts`): 10 new tickets per user per hour, 60 comments per user per hour.

### 2.4 Staff (elevated platform workspace)

- **Queue** `GET /platform/tickets` with filters: status, priority, category, assignee (me, unassigned, anyone), customer, and search by number or subject; paginated, oldest-first within priority by default. Needs `ticket.read`.
- **Detail** `GET /platform/tickets/:id`: everything, including internal notes and internal attachments. Reads are audited (ADR-010).
- **Commands:** triage (sets category and priority), start, await customer, hold/resume, resolve, reopen; change priority (reason required) and category; link an order. Needs `ticket.comment` for public replies and `ticket.internal_note` for notes.
- **Ownership (P8-1):** "Take" assigns the ticket to yourself (any staff with `ticket.read` and `ticket.comment`); assigning to someone else or unassigning needs `ticket.assign`. The assignee list is active PLATFORM members holding `ticket.comment`.
- **On behalf (P8-6):** `POST /platform/tickets` with `ticket.create` (helpdesk, customer support, service and ICT support managers). Staff pick the customer through a new `GET /platform/customers?q=` lookup (organisations by name, people by exact email; organisations then list their active members as the requester). The ticket records `on_behalf`, `created_by` and the channel; the customer is emailed.
- **Requests on behalf (ADR-047, P8-7):** `POST /platform/requests` with a new `platform.request.on_behalf` permission (helpdesk, customer support, service manager, super admin). It reuses the Phase 7 submission path with the same price snapshot and audience rules, records `created_by` staff and an `on_behalf` flag, and widens the `protect_service_request` trigger only for that insert. Quotations are still accepted by the customer.

### 2.6 Notifications (outbox → email)

- **Customer** (requester; org admins with `ticket.read_all` on P1 tickets): ticket received; new staff reply; waiting for you; resolved (with the reopen deadline); closed; opened on your behalf.
- **Staff:** ticket assigned to you; customer replied on your ticket.
- Emails never include internal-note text (tested with a canary string).

## 6. Decisions needed before starting

| ID   | Question                                            | Recommendation                                                                                                                                                                                           | Why                                                                                                                                        |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| P8-1 | Who "owns" a ticket in Phase 8?                     | **One OGS staff assignee per ticket.** Any agent can "Take" a ticket; assigning others needs `ticket.assign`. Dispatching work to technicians/providers stays a separate `ServiceAssignment` in Phase 9. | Helpdesk agents lack `ticket.assign` but must pick up work; keeping the helpdesk owner separate from field dispatch avoids redesign later. |
| P8-2 | What is the reopen policy?                          | **Customers may reopen within 7 days of resolution (reason required); staff may reopen until closed; auto-close after 7 days; CLOSED is final, and a follow-up is a new linked ticket.**                 | A clear window satisfies review §13, keeps reporting honest (Phase 10/13 count reopens) and stops years-old tickets reviving.              |
| P8-3 | How is priority set?                                | **The customer chooses urgency; staff set the priority (P1–P4) at triage and may change it with a reason.** Both are stored.                                                                             | Customers over-rate urgency; Phase 10 SLA targets key on the staff-confirmed priority.                                                     |
| P8-4 | Who in an organisation sees which tickets?          | **Own by default; new `ticket.read_department` adds the member's department (not granted by default); new `ticket.read_all` for admins.** The department is snapshotted on the ticket at creation.       | It delivers the matrix's "own/dept" with the ADR-054 pattern, and admins choose who gets department visibility.                            |
| P8-5 | How are internal notes stored?                      | **A separate `ticket_internal_note` table whose RLS has no customer branch**, rather than a visibility flag on comments.                                                                                 | A flag relies on every query filtering correctly; a separate table makes the leak impossible at the database layer (review §11).           |
| P8-6 | How do staff open tickets on behalf of customers?   | **`ticket.create` in the platform workspace, under elevation, with a customer lookup, a recorded channel and an `on_behalf` flag; the customer is emailed.**                                             | Phone and walk-in support is common in Kenya; the flag and audit row answer "who raised this?".                                            |
| P8-7 | Do staff also place service requests on behalf now? | **Yes, deferred here by ADR-047:** new `platform.request.on_behalf`, same Phase 7 rules and price snapshot, the customer still accepts any quotation.                                                    | The helpdesk and its customer lookup now exist, which is what ADR-047 was waiting for; the customer keeps the commercial decision.         |

## 7. Risks

- **Internal-note leak.** Mitigation: separate table without a customer RLS branch, separate DTOs, the canary contract test over every customer route and email, and a distinct visual treatment plus explicit button labels in the staff composer.
- **State machine sprawl.** Mitigation: one transition table drives code, triggers and T1.
- **Widening the Phase 7 request trigger for on-behalf inserts.** Mitigation: the widening is limited to elevated staff with the new permission and the `on_behalf` flag; F1–F9 rerun unchanged.
- **Attachment abuse.** Mitigation: existing purpose limits, scan quarantine, rate limits.
- **Phase 6b not yet merged.** Mitigation: Phase 8 is cut from `main` only after 6b merges, so the new screens build on the final design system.

## 8. Verification (how to check the phase end to end)

1. `pnpm infra:up`; ask the owner to run `pnpm db:reset:test` if the test database needs resetting (never run `migrate reset` from the agent).
2. `pnpm check`, `pnpm test:integration` (T1–T8, RLS catalogue, isolation), `pnpm --filter @ogs/api build` then `pnpm test:e2e` (T9, visual, axe, motif).
3. Manual run: as an org employee open a ticket with a PDF; as helpdesk elevate, take, add an internal note, reply; confirm the employee's view and email show only the reply; resolve, reopen, accept.
